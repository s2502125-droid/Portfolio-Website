ABOUT ME

Music has been a defining part of my life for more than fourteen years. What began as a childhood curiosity developed into a lifelong passion for performance, artistic expression, and continuous learning.


My experience spans solo recitals, ensemble collaborations, and community events, allowing me to connect with audiences through a wide variety of musical styles.


While classical music remains my primary focus, I also enjoy arranging and performing modern pieces that bring familiar melodies to life through the violin.
